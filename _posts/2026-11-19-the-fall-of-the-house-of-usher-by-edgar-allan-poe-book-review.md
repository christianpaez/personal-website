I decided to listen to an Everand audiobook of this title since the last time I actually read it was back in school (they forced us to do so), so I did not remember much of this book at the time, but given the fact that it is very popular, I gave it a go.

### Small Scenario, Big Details

Most of this story happens in a short scenario. Well, it’s a big house, but the author makes a great job at making use of the environment’s details despite it not offering too much. This seems to be a pattern from Poe since I felt something similar in *The Raven*.

### Characters are actually scary and insane

There are not many characters in this book, but they are very memorable, for being crazy, mad and just weird. The author does not take it lightly when portraying them. I would not say this is a scary book in a raw physical sense, but we get the sense that the minds of the characters are in a creepy place, kinda like Resident Evil vs Silent Hill in some way.

### Intensity and madness increase at a good step

This is another common pattern from Poe. You get introduced to some scenario and its creepiness intensifies throughout the book, and you get to the point where you are at the last dozen pages and you cannot stop reading.

## Recommended?

1000%, I read this like 3 years ago, and I still remember being very hooked at the last couple of pages, really immense read/listen.

I literally paid pennies for this book at a secondhand book event. It seemed bold, offensive and kinda short, so I decided to give it a read. Note: Does not have an English version, sadly.

## A lot of topics to choose from

This book is basically a collection of concepts and ideas about all the topics we care about as humans. I can guarantee you, no matter what your background or beliefs are, you will find in this book an interesting topic to laugh at or be offended at. It literally goes over politics, economics, general culture, religion and more.

## Funny without filtering

The author tries to be funny without fear of being bold. The book is not a deep read, it is not about learning too many new things, but mostly about making fun of how smart and dumb we as humans can be. I think the author is actually funny, by the way.

## Author did not hold back, you might be offended

Since the book discusses literally everything we kinda know as humans (that’s why it’s called a dictionary), you will probably be offended at some point, whether you believe in capitalism or angels. I was offended during the science and psychology discussions, but I did not take myself too seriously, so it was enjoyable.

## Worth a read?

I think it is, it is genuinely nice to feel offended in a nonchalant way. The book is short and cheap, so why not? You have to read it in Spanish or Italian, I think, so if you do not speak a second language, this is a good excuse.

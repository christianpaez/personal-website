This is one of the first audiobooks that created the desire to not always read stuff on paper or e-readers. I remember the atmosphere of this story was greatly enhanced by its audio production. I cannot remember the studio who recorded this but it was an Everand title. Also, this was kinda special since it allowed me to discover the story behind Roman Dirge’s Lenore character.

## Great psychological plot

The book deals with grief as an emotion which works very well as a psychological plot, since it’s a very complex subject, most of us can relate to it and probably everyone deals with it in a different way. Even if you do not relate to this feeling, the thought of it will touch you mentally.

## Simple environment

The poem’s location is just a room and the main character’s mind, so this is a text that does not need you to be super mindful about details and plot. Everything is straight to the point and psychological.

## Drama with small items

The plot also involves a very small number of objects. Surprisingly, now that I write this, I notice that everything happens in the readers’ and main character’s minds. This is probably one of the most mentally hitting books I have read.

## Do I recommend?

I do, especially if you can get a nice audiobook version, the atmosphere just adds so much to this poem.

This is another small title I found when I was looking for drawing 
references for small flower ink drawings. Just like the other reference 
book I used in the past, this was extremely handy.

## Great detail on images

The drawings present in this book have a great amount of attention to 
detail, something I appreciate a lot since that was the main reason I 
got this title, to find stuff so I could sketch.

## Black and white: Shading is explicit

The main mediums I was using at the time were pencil and ink, usually 
monochrome. Most of the images in this title are black and white, so 
having explicit shading and highlights was just what I needed for my 
drawings.

## Great mix of architecture, interior design and art

My old art teacher used to tell me how boring it is to draw still life and
 flowers(I think he was right), but the book contained a decent mixture 
of ornaments in different mediums and scenery. It was not all art-based,
 but also a good number of references were focused on interior design, 
great variety in general.

## Do I have a motive to recommend this?

Yes,f absolutely. Abstract ornaments and designs do a great job at producing 
inspiration and motivating you to try some different kinds of drawing.

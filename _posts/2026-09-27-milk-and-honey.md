I found this title in audiobook format in Everand. I just saw that it was poetry and the title seemed interesting. I do not usually go for poetry and was willing to explore. At some point, I realized it was probably a book for a female listener/reader, but the book was very intimate, so I carried on with it.

## A simplistic approach to emotion and drama

The audiobook is read by the author, and she transmits a massive amount of emotion during the read. Even though there aren’t sound effects, her voice feels very vulnerable, honest and intimate. That was definitely a plus for authors reading their own audiobooks, since they are the ones who relate to it the most.

## Mundane scenario and ambience

Most of the book takes places in day-to-day scenarios. This seems to be an inside look into the common young female. I think that made the storytelling very relatable and easy to picture even though I am not a woman.

## Hidden demons of the modern woman

The deepest insecurities and self-doubt of modern females are portrayed in this book. It was interesting to me being exposed to something that I probably did not think was so vast, personal and emotional for what I can imagine is a common situation for a lot of ladies in the world.

## Was it good?

I enjoyed the title. Even though I did not read the printed book, I would recommend the audiobook over it. The author does a great job reading it. It’s a good and short poetry title.

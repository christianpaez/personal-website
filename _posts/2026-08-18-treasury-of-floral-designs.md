At some point, I was a heavy Scribd user and found this title there. Since I was looking for some simple images to do pen sketches, I started to skim this book and was pleasantly surprised.

## A sketching resource that is easy to use

I have used Google Images, Pinterest and the like to find images and references to draw, but sometimes it is hard to filter drawing style, photographs, angles, etc. As the book implies, this contains floral designs in general, so finding a good resource does not take a long time.

## Simple graphics, easy to get inspired by

The content of the book is great for sketches, pen drawing and tattoos. Some of the designs look elaborate enough to be used in composition studies and the like; it was almost impossible not to find something to draw whenever I was looking for drawing references.

## Non-repetitive content

Despite being only a flower-based book, it has a good amount of variety in composition, shading and design. Ignoring the fact that most graphics used a monochrome palette, this collection did not seem too repetitive to me.

## Good or bad?

This is a great resource for fun sketching or just compositions whenever working on designs with a monochrome palette. It does not have too many colored examples.
